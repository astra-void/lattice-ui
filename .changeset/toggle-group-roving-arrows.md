`ToggleGroup.Root` takes an `orientation` prop (`"horizontal"` by default, or `"vertical"`), and arrow keys on a focused item move focus to the neighbouring enabled item in layout order, the way `Tabs` and `RadioGroup` rove. Items used to handle only Return/Space. Arrows move focus only: an item is still pressed by activation, not by receiving focus.
